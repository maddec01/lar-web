Collection lists and tables no longer draw as boxed, ruled tables. A VirtualList sits unboxed on the page under a quiet
muted column header that still sticks while scrolling; its rows (DataRow) lose their hairlines and take a rounded fill
under the pointer, with focus inside and around a focused row's link. Section headers in a list lose their rule, and
DataTable drops its header line and row rules for slightly roomier rows.
