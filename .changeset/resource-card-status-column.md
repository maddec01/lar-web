ResourceCard (default) is a container: its status sits beside the details from 36rem in a column of at most half the card, its children clamped so a long status truncates or wraps instead of painting over the details, and below 36rem it takes its own line under the details, from their text's start. The footer's trailing controls keep to the end when they wrap.
